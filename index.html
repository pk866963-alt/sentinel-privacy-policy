Privacy Policy for Sentinel: Void Defense

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and disclosure of information obtained from users of the mobile application Sentinel: Void Defense ("the Game"), created and maintained by the independent developer ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require personal identification (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number) to play. However, third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) integrated into the application automatically collect technical device data:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID, Unique Device Identifiers (UDID), IP addresses.
  • Gameplay & Analytics Data: Player progress, virtual currency balance, game settings, and session statistics.
  • Diagnostic Information: Application crash logs, performance metrics, and system diagnostic data.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We utilize trusted third-party service providers to power game infrastructure, cloud saves, and monetization. These services process data under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Serves rewarded video advertisements and manages ad targeting. Google Privacy & Terms
  • Unity Gaming Services (UGS): Handles cloud save synchronization, player profile identification, and game telemetry. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Manages store services, achievements, and core mobile Android framework APIs.

3. How We Use Collected Information

The collected technical data is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • To ensure seamless gameplay mechanics and store/restore your game progress in the cloud.
  • To deliver rewarded video advertisements when requested by the player.
  • To monitor app performance, identify technical bugs, and optimize stability across mobile devices.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

The Game is designed and intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with technical personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data from our third-party infrastructure.

5. Data Retention and User Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your game telemetry data. You may reset your Android Advertising ID at any time via your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID).
